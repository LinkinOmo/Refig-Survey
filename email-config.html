✉️ Email Delivery — Brevo Fallback

Configure the backup email channel used when the Gmail daily quota runs out

Back to App

When the Gmail daily quota (~100 recipients/day) is exhausted, app emails (survey notifications, work-order approvals, safety incidents…) are sent through the Brevo transactional API instead. The sender email must be a verified sender in your Brevo account.

Paste your xkeysib-… key. Leave blank to keep the saved key.

The API key is stored in this app's Script Properties only — never exposed to the browser after saving.
