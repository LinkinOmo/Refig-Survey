How roles work here: "Admin" and "Super Admin" are the same underlying flag (both grant full access everywhere, always). "System Admin" is a separate, narrower tier that can open the config pages below but does not get survey-edit rights automatically. "SMF" and "Operation" are not roles stored per-user — they are audiences the toggles below apply to (SMF = tagged "SMF" in Employee_Database's Note column; Operation = everyone else who's logged in).
